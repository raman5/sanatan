Bhakti

Bhakti app · data deletion

You can ask us to delete your Bhakti account and the data we hold about you at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. If you have an active subscription, cancel it first in the app: Profile → Subscription → Cancel subscription.
  2. Send an email to maneeshanegi30@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Bhakti account", from the Google account (Gmail address) you use to sign in to Bhakti.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Bhakti account: name, email address and Google account ID.
  • Your subscription record: plan, status and dates.

What is kept

  • Payment records held by Razorpay, which must be kept as required by Indian law. They are not used for anything else.

Your routine, japa counts and progress are stored only on your phone. Uninstall the app or clear its data to remove them.

Contact for any privacy question: maneeshanegi30@gmail.com
