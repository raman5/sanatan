Everything for your daily practice

Bhakti turns devotion into a simple routine you can open every morning. Pooja, japa, mantra, darshan, done.

🛕

Daily Pooja

Step into a virtual shrine for your ishta devata and offer your pooja right from your phone.

  • Ring the temple bell and fold your hands
  • Shower flowers, offer water and milk
  • Each ritual with its own animation and sound
📿

Naam Japa

Choose a deity and repeat their name on an animated 108-bead mala.

  • Every tap chanted aloud in Hindi, or silent if you prefer
  • Today's count, malas completed and lifetime total
  • A 🔥 streak to keep you coming back
🕉️

Mantras with meaning

Each mantra comes with the Devanagari text, transliteration and a plain-language explanation, plus its own japa counter that chants with you.

📈

See your consistency

Your routine resets every morning. The Progress tab shows how regularly you've kept it over the last three months.

  • Current and best streak
  • Monday-to-Sunday view with daily completion
  • A monthly calendar that lights up every full day
🖼️ Wallpapers

Deity wallpapers for Shiva, Krishna, Ram, Hanuman, Ganesh, Durga, Lakshmi and more. Set or save in one tap.

💬 WhatsApp Status

Good morning, good night and festival status images, ready to share with family.

🔒 No account needed

Just tell Bhakti your name. Your routine and japa progress stay on your phone.
