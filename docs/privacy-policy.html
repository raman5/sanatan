Bhakti

Last updated 15 October 2026

Bhakti is developed and maintained by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This page explains what data the app handles, why, and who processes it. Questions any time: maneeshanegi30@gmail.com

The short version

  • You sign in with Google so your subscription stays linked to you. We receive your name, email address and a Google account ID.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Your routine, japa counts and progress stay on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile ID with us through Firebase Authentication. We use them to identify your account and link it to your subscription.
  • Subscription information. Your plan (monthly or annual), its status (for example active or cancelled), the end date of the current billing period, and the Razorpay subscription ID. We use this to unlock the app and to show your plan in Profile.
  • Payment details are collected by Razorpay, not by us. When you subscribe, Razorpay's checkout collects what it needs to process the payment and set up AutoPay (such as your phone number, email, and UPI, card or bank details) under Razorpay's privacy policy. Razorpay sends us only the payment status and IDs needed to confirm your subscription.

What stays on your phone

  • Your routine choices, japa counts, streaks, the last three months of progress, favourites, notification settings and language.
  • A profile photo, if you choose one. It is stored in the app's private storage and never uploaded.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. We cannot see it or restore it.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage) hosts your account and subscription record and delivers the app's wallpapers, mantras and sounds. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Razorpay processes subscription payments and AutoPay mandates. See Razorpay's privacy policy.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the service. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

Device permissions

  • Notifications: only for the daily reminders you switch on.
  • Set wallpaper: only when you tap "Set as wallpaper".
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save an image to your gallery when you tap save.

Chanting aloud uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. No microphone access is requested.

Security

All data sent between the app, Firebase and Razorpay is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your subscription record can only be read by your own signed-in account and written by our payment server.

Retention and deleting your account

We keep your account and subscription record while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and all data we hold about you at any time. See how to delete your account. Razorpay keeps payment records for as long as Indian law requires, even after your account is deleted.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or use analytics, tracking or advertising IDs.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, messages, or photos other than one you pick yourself.

Children's privacy

Bhakti is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any change will be shown by a new "Last updated" date above.
